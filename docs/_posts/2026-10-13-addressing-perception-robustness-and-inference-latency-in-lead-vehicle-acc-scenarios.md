Expected goals:

- Mitigating perception degradation from vehicle shadows and horizon lane-merging issues when a lead vehicle is present.
- Improving perception latency and throughput (FPS) for high-speed scenarios (e.g. 50 km/h) by exploring Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) networks and optimizing image preprocessing pipelines.
- Investigating CARLA-specific retraining and tuning (higher resolution input, ROI adjustment) while evaluating alternatives or maintaining YOLOP.
